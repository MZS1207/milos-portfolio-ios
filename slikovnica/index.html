Little hands, big colors.

A calm coloring and drawing space for children aged 5 and under, on iPhone and iPad.

10 free coloring pages · Free sketchpad · No ads

Color and draw

Choose a color, then tap an area of a coloring page. Slide the palette sideways for all 14 colors. Pinch to zoom and move with two fingers. My sketchpad lets you draw with a finger or Apple Pencil using a pencil, marker and eraser.

Saved drawings

Coloring pages and one current freehand drawing save automatically on this device. Use My drawings to continue coloring. Undo and redo are available. Start again and New paper ask before clearing a drawing.

Collections and purchases

Five optional collections contain 20 pages each: Dinosaurs, Planes and cars, Fairies, Animals and Dolls. Each collection is a one-time purchase; the Complete Collection unlocks all 100 paid pages. Previews are free. There are no subscriptions. The App Store shows the final local price before purchase.

Restore a purchase

Open the lock on the home screen, complete the parental check, choose Explore collections and then Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. An internet connection is required; restoring an existing purchase does not charge again.

Language and sound

In the parent area, follow the device language or choose English, Spanish or Serbian. Music and spoken audio are not available in this version.
