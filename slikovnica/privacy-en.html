Privacy policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

App data

Slikovnica has no user accounts, advertising or third-party analytics. We do not request children’s names, birthdays, location, contacts, camera or microphone access. Colored pages, one freehand drawing and settings are stored locally on the device and are not sent to a developer server.

Apple purchases

Apple processes payments and restoration through the App Store. The app checks Apple transaction information to unlock purchased collections. The developer does not receive payment card details. Apple processes information under its own privacy policy.

Storage and deletion

Use Start again to clear a coloring page or New paper to clear the sketchpad, after confirmation. Deleting the app removes its local data. Device backups may retain a copy; manage those backups in Apple settings. Deleting drawings does not cancel purchases.

Support

If a parent contacts us, their email address and the information they voluntarily provide are used to answer the request. Please do not send children’s personal data. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any legally required retention. Email is handled through Apple iCloud Mail.

This website

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no analytics scripts, advertising, forms or tracking cookies added by us. GitHub may process technical access information, such as an IP address, to provide and secure hosting. Its privacy statement explains this processing.
