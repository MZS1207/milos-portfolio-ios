Privacy policy

Pet Buddy: Care Diary is a local pet care diary developed by Miloš Stevanović. No developer account or sign-in is required.

Your diary stays on your device

Pet profiles, care calendar entries, measurements, health notes, documents and photos are stored locally. The developer does not receive or operate a server holding your diary. The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps.

Permissions and Apple services

You choose which photos and files to attach. Camera and notification permissions are optional. Apple Maps search and directions use Apple services under Apple's privacy policy. Plus purchases and restoration are processed by Apple through StoreKit. The app uses verified transaction information to unlock purchased features; the developer does not receive your payment card details.

Backups, transfer and sharing

You can export an encrypted backup and transfer it yourself, or share a PDF report or document with a recipient you select. Keep backup files and their password safe. Files you choose to send leave your device through the destination you select and are subject to that service's practices. Device backups may also be managed by your iOS and iCloud settings.

Control and deletion

You can edit and delete diary records in the app. Removing the app can remove its local data. Export a backup before removing it or changing phones. Delete exported copies separately from wherever you saved or shared them.

Support and children

If you contact support, the email and information you voluntarily provide are used to answer your request. Do not send private diary records unless necessary. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
