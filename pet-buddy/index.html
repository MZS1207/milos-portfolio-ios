Pet Buddy support

A calm place for your pets, care calendar and everyday records. Available in English and Serbian.

Move to a new phone

In Settings, open Backup and transfer. Export an encrypted backup, save its password separately and transfer the file to the new phone. Install Pet Buddy, open Backup and transfer, choose the file and enter its password. Review the import confirmation before proceeding. Keep the original backup until you have verified your pets, records and attachments.

Restore Plus

Plus is a one-time in-app purchase. Use the same Apple Account as the original purchase and select Restore Purchases on the Plus screen. Prices are supplied by the App Store in your local currency.

Veterinary reports

Open a pet's health report to generate and share a basic PDF. Plus adds period and section selection and supported document attachments. Pet Buddy is a diary and educational tool; it does not diagnose or replace veterinary care.
