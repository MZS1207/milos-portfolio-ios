Privacy Policy

Updated 12 October 2026

In short

We do not collect any data from Nebnik. The app has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it does not use any server of ours. It does not send your data anywhere.

Your birth data and your dream journal stay only on your device.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter stays only on your device: your birth date, time and place, the sign you chose, your settings and notification times, your dream journal, your favorites and your reading progress. The app keeps these in its own private folder on the device.

The sky calculations, the daily reading, search and the recognition of symbols in your dream journal all run on the device. We cannot see any of this data, and it is never sent to us or to anyone else.

The birth place is chosen from a list of cities built into the app or typed in as coordinates. The app does not use your location.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, the operating system may include the app’s folder in that backup. The backup is handled by Apple or by your computer under their own terms, and we have no access to it.

How to delete your data

You can delete your birth data in Profile, on the birth data form, and you can delete dream journal entries one by one. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device.

Notifications

Notifications are local: the app schedules them on the device, and they do not pass through any server. They never contain your name or your birth data.

You can turn them off in the app or in the system settings at any time. The app works fully without them.

Purchases

Purchases are made through the App Store and processed by Apple. The app only learns from the system on your device which purchases are active, so that it can unlock content.

We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. What Apple does with payment data is Apple’s own processing under its privacy policy, not ours.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data. The only permission it may ask for is notifications, and only after you tap to turn them on.

Children

The app is not directed at children. It does not collect data from anyone, children included, so we hold no data about children.

Contact

Developer and contact: Milos Stevanovic. Email: m.stevanovic1207@icloud.com.

If you write to us by email, we use your message and address only to reply to you. We do not add them to any list and we do not share them with anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes the way it handles data, we will update this policy before the change takes effect. The date of the last change is shown at the top. This policy is also published at https://mzs1207.github.io/milos-portfolio-ios/nebnik/privacy.html.

Website hosting

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request data to provide hosting. The website has no analytics, advertising or contact form. Email support is separate from the app; information you choose to send is used to answer your request.
