Nebnik support

For app questions, purchase problems or feedback, email the developer. Include your app version, iOS version and a short description of the issue. Do not send birth data, dream journal entries or payment details.

m.stevanovic1207@icloud.com

Purchases and restoration

Use Restore Purchases in Profile or on the purchase screen. Use the same Apple Account as the original purchase. Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions. Apple handles payments and refund requests.

Apple: Report a Problem

Your data

Birth data, preferences and dream journal entries are stored locally. Deleting the app removes its local data; device backups may retain a copy. Restoring a purchase does not restore deleted journal entries.
